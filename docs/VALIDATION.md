## Analysis of the failures (written after opening, not pre-registered)

10 of 20 contrasts missed 0.30, and they fall in two groups. The other 10 were all within 0.30.

- **Semaglutide phase 2, 12 weeks:** all 6 contrasts over-predicted benefit, by 0.44 to 0.65. The 56-week SUSTAIN 2 contrasts were within 0.12. The rival missed here too (MAE 0.478).
- **Metformin arms of the factorial trial:** all 4 under-predicted benefit, by 0.31 to 0.56. Metformin was calibrated only through one difference against sitagliptin, with no placebo anchor, and this trial's HbA1c range was not in its registry record, so 7 to 11 was assumed.

Possible causes, none tested: the model may reach full effect too early in short trials, and metformin's effect may be under-identified or the factorial population mis-specified. V6 failed overall, so across all 20 contrasts the physiology did not earn its complexity against carrying effects forward. Its clearest gain was in the kidney strata, but that is a small post-hoc subgroup, one with a thin pool.

No parameter was changed after opening. A fix would make these trials non-blind and would need fresh held-out trials to test, so the result above stands as the lab's reported performance.
